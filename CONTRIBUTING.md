## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Download the latest release.
2. Launch the application.
3. Create your player profile.
4. Select your preferred role.
5. Add your favorite heroes.
6. Configure your competitive goals.
7. Create hero and composition presets.
8. Start recording match results.
9. Review your statistics and performance trends.
10. Adjust your strategies based on your personal data.

## 📊 Recommended Workflow

### Before a Match

- Select your role
- Review map notes
- Check composition presets
- Review hero priorities
- Prepare strategy notes

### During Your Session

- Record match results
- Track hero selections
- Record important observations
- Update performance notes

### After Your Session

- Review statistics
- Check win rate
- Compare heroes
- Review map performance
- Update strategy presets
- Set the next improvement goal
